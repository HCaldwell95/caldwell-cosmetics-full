Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1st April 2024

Caldwell Cosmetics is committed to protecting your privacy and personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our website, book treatments, or purchase products.

Information We Collect

  • Personal details (name, contact details, date of birth)
  • Medical information relevant to treatments
  • Treatment records and consultation notes
  • Payment and booking information

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to:

  • Provide safe and effective aesthetic treatments
  • Manage bookings and client records

Data Protection & Security

All personal and medical information is stored securely and accessed only by authorised personnel. We comply with UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

You have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data, subject to legal retention requirements.
