Our Signature Service

Your Bespoke Skin Journey

Not a one-size-fits-all appointment — a fully personalised roadmap built around your skin, your goals, and your story.

The Bespoke Skin Journey is the cornerstone of everything I do at Caldwell Cosmetics. Rather than booking isolated treatments, we begin with a thorough skin assessment — and from there, build a plan that is entirely yours.

What's Included
◎

In-Depth Assessment

We begin with a comprehensive skin consultation — discussing your concerns, history, lifestyle, and goals in full. Nothing is rushed and nothing is overlooked.

◈

Your Skin Roadmap

Based on your assessment, I design a structured treatment plan — a roadmap that sequences and spaces treatments to give you the best possible results over time.

◉

Full Photo Documentation

Every stage of your journey is professionally photographed so we can track real progress and celebrate the changes you might stop noticing over time.

◇

Clinical Notes & Portfolio

Detailed notes are taken throughout — building a living portfolio of your skin journey that informs every decision we make together at every stage.

◆

Regular Progress Reviews

As we move through your plan, we review and adapt. Your skin changes — and so does your roadmap, whenever it needs to.

✦

Ongoing Support

You are never left to manage alone between appointments. Guidance, aftercare advice, and product recommendations are all part of the process.

How It Works
01

Initial Consultation

We sit down together and I take the time to understand your skin history, concerns, and what you would like to achieve. This is an open, unhurried, pressure-free conversation.

02

Clinical Skin Assessment

A thorough assessment of your skin is carried out — examining texture, pigmentation, hydration, sensitivity, and any areas of concern — giving us a clear baseline to build from.

03

Your Roadmap is Built

I design a fully bespoke treatment plan. This includes which treatments to use, in what order, and how frequently — everything sequenced to give you the best possible outcome.

04

Treatments Begin

We start your plan at the right pace for your skin. Before and after photographs are taken, clinical notes are recorded, and every session builds thoughtfully on the last.

05

Progress Reviews & Adaptation

At regular intervals, we review your progress together, compare photographs, and adjust the plan if needed. This is your journey — and it evolves with you throughout.
