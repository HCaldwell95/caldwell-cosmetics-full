About Us

Our Philosophy

At Caldwell Cosmetics, we believe beauty is personal. We encourage treatments and self-care as a way to enhance your own features, not to meet someone else's standards. Our focus is on helping people feel confident, comfortable, and authentic in their own skin.

Meet Rabia, our Lead Nurse. With over seven years of experience, Rabia combines professional expertise with a genuine passion for holistic well-being. Her compassionate approach ensures that every treatment is safe, effective, and perfectly tailored to you.

We specialise in personalised treatments designed to bring out your natural glow, all within a calm, welcoming space that encourages self-care. Every detail of your visit is thoughtfully considered, creating a bespoke experience that feels as good as it looks.
