Botulinum Toxin — Pre-Treatment Advice

Please read and follow this advice before your appointment to help achieve the best possible result and reduce the risk of complications.

  1. Arrive with a clean face. The treatment area should be free from makeup, moisturiser, perfume, and any other skincare products.
  2. Avoid blood-thinning medications such as aspirin, ibuprofen (Nurofen/Motrin), naproxen, and Vitamin E supplements for at least 48 hours before your appointment, unless they are prescribed by your GP. Do not stop any prescribed medication without consulting your doctor first.
  3. Avoid alcohol for at least 24 hours before your treatment. Alcohol can thin the blood and increase the risk of bruising.
  4. Avoid fish oil and high-dose Omega-3 supplements for at least 48 hours prior to treatment, as these may also increase bruising risk.
  5. Avoid facial treatments such as facials, chemical peels, microdermabrasion, laser, or IPL for at least 2 weeks before your appointment.
  6. Avoid dental procedures in the 2 weeks before your Botulinum Toxin treatment where possible.
  7. If you are prone to cold sores, please inform your practitioner in advance so that preventative medication can be considered.
  8. Eat a light meal and stay well hydrated before your appointment. Arriving on an empty stomach is not recommended.
  9. Inform your practitioner of any changes to your health, medications, or supplements since your last visit or since completing your consultation form.
  10. Arrive on time and try to feel relaxed. Stress and rushing can increase the likelihood of bruising at injection sites.

Important Reminders

  • Please do not wear contact lenses to your appointment if you are having treatment near the eye area.
  • If you are pregnant, breastfeeding, or trying to conceive, please notify your practitioner — Botulinum Toxin is not suitable during this time.
  • Let your practitioner know if you have had any recent vaccinations, infections, or illness.
