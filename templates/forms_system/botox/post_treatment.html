Botulinum Toxin — Post-Treatment Advice

Please follow this advice carefully after your treatment to ensure the best results and minimise the risk of side effects.

  1. Remain upright for at least 4 hours after your treatment. Do not lie down, bend forward, or tilt your head downward during this period.
  2. Do not touch, rub, or apply pressure to the treated area for at least 4 hours. This includes pillows, helmets, and tight headwear.
  3. Avoid strenuous exercise, saunas, steam rooms, and hot baths for at least 24 hours following treatment. Increased blood flow and heat can affect where the toxin settles.
  4. Avoid alcohol for at least 24 hours after treatment to reduce bruising and swelling.
  5. Avoid applying makeup to the treated area for at least 4 hours. When you do reapply, use gentle dabbing motions rather than rubbing.
  6. Avoid facial treatments (massages, facials, microdermabrasion, chemical peels, laser, or IPL) for at least 2 weeks after treatment.
  7. Avoid dental procedures for at least 2 weeks following your treatment where possible.
  8. Mild redness, bruising, or swelling at the injection sites is normal and should subside within a few days. A cold compress (not ice directly on the skin) may help to reduce swelling.
  9. Results are not immediate. The full effect of Botulinum Toxin typically develops over 3–14 days. Please do not assess your results before your 2-week review.
  10. 2-week review: A complimentary review is offered to assess your results. Please contact the clinic if you have any concerns before this appointment. Top-up injections are only provided free of charge within this 2-week window.
  11. Protect the treated area from direct sun exposure and always apply SPF30+ when outdoors.
  12. Avoid blood-thinning medications (unless prescribed) for 24 hours after treatment to minimise bruising.

When to Contact Us

Please contact the clinic promptly if you experience any of the following after treatment:

  • Signs of an allergic reaction (hives, widespread redness, difficulty breathing)
  • Significant drooping of an eyelid or brow that does not resolve within a few days
  • Blurred or double vision
  • Difficulty swallowing or speaking
  • Any symptoms that feel severe or are worsening
