Laser & IPL — Pre-Treatment Advice

Please read and follow this advice before every laser or IPL appointment to ensure your treatment is safe and effective.

  1. Arrive with a clean treatment area. The area to be treated should be completely free from makeup, perfume, moisturiser, deodorant, body or face creams, and any other cosmetic products.
  2. Avoid waxing, plucking, threading, hair removal creams, and bleaching in the treatment area for at least 7 days before treatment. Shaving is acceptable.
  3. Stop use of topical retinoids and other strong cosmetic actives (e.g. retinol, tretinoin) for at least 2 weeks before treatment. Stop use of glycolic acid or other AHA/BHA products for at least 7 days before treatment.
  4. No active or fake tan is permitted in the treatment area. Use of sunbeds or prolonged sun exposure is not allowed in the month prior to treatment. Suntans must fade completely before treatment can begin. Fake tans must also be fully removed.
  5. Use SPF30+ daily on the treatment area between appointments (SPF50 is recommended). We recommend Lynton Light Protect Daily Defence SPF50. Failure to protect from the sun between sessions can increase the risk of adverse reactions.
  6. Inform us of any changes to your medical history or medications since your last visit or consultation form, including any medications bought from a pharmacy or herbalist.
  7. Arrive on time for your appointment. This will allow us to pre-cool the skin before treatment if required.
  8. Avoid recent cosmetic injectables (Botulinum Toxin, dermal fillers) in or near the treatment area for at least 2 weeks prior to laser treatment, unless advised otherwise.

Please Note

  • We reserve the right to charge for any appointment that is booked but must be abandoned due to an active suntan or the presence of fake tan.
  • If you are pregnant or breastfeeding, please inform us before your appointment — some laser treatments are not suitable during this time.
  • If you have had any recent illness, infection, or changes to your skin since your last visit, please let us know before treatment begins.
  • If you have any questions or concerns before your appointment, please contact the clinic.
