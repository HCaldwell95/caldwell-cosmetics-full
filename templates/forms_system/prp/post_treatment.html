PRP — Post-Treatment Advice

Please follow this advice carefully after your treatment to support healing and achieve the best possible results.

  1. Redness, swelling, and bruising at the treatment site are common and expected. These are a normal part of the healing response and typically resolve within 3–5 days.
  2. Do not touch or apply pressure to the treated area for at least 4 hours after treatment.
  3. Avoid applying makeup or skincare products to the treated area for at least 24 hours. After this time, resume your routine gently.
  4. Avoid strenuous exercise, saunas, steam rooms, hot baths, and hot showers for at least 24 hours after treatment.
  5. Avoid alcohol for at least 24 hours following treatment.
  6. Do not take anti-inflammatory medications such as aspirin or ibuprofen for at least 48 hours after treatment. These can inhibit the inflammatory healing response that PRP relies on to work effectively. Paracetamol may be used if pain relief is needed.
  7. Keep the treated area moisturised and protected from the sun. Apply SPF30+ whenever outdoors, and avoid direct sun exposure on the treated area.
  8. Avoid retinoids and strong active skincare (e.g. retinol, glycolic acid, AHAs/BHAs) for at least 5–7 days after treatment on the treated area.
  9. Avoid chemical peels, laser treatments, microneedling, or other cosmetic procedures for at least 2 weeks after your PRP treatment.
  10. Results are gradual. Improvement is typically seen from 4–6 weeks after treatment, with continued improvement developing over 3–6 months. Multiple treatments may be required to achieve optimal results. Do not judge your results too soon.
  11. Avoid smoking for at least 24 hours after treatment, as nicotine can impair the healing and regenerative response.

When to Contact Us

Please contact the clinic promptly if you experience any of the following:

  • Signs of infection — increasing redness, warmth, pain, swelling, or discharge at the treatment site
  • Fever or generally feeling unwell after treatment
  • Severe or prolonged bruising or swelling that does not begin to improve after 5–7 days
  • Any symptoms that feel severe, unusual, or are worsening
