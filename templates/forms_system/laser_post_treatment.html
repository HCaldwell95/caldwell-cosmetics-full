Laser & IPL — Post-Treatment Advice

Please follow this advice carefully after every laser or IPL session to support healing and protect the treated area.

  1. Cool the area if needed. In order to minimise skin heating and reduce the likelihood of reactions, the treatment area may be cooled before, during, and after the procedure. If the area still feels hot at home, apply a cold flannel or cool pack. Do not allow ice packs or ice to come into direct contact with the skin.
  2. Mild reactions are normal. Following treatment, you may experience mild stinging, dryness, itching, mild grazing, flakiness, sensitivity to UV, or slight redness. This is completely normal and should settle over the next few days.
  3. Swelling or bruising can occasionally occur and may last anywhere from 1–2 days up to 1–2 weeks. If treating a facial area, sleeping with your head raised on pillows will minimise swelling. Cool packs will also help. If your skin is particularly sensitive and prone to histamine reactions, consider taking an antihistamine.
  4. Following vascular treatments, a bruise may appear at the treated area. This may last up to 15 days. As the bruise fades, there may be brown discolouration of the skin, which usually resolves within 1–3 months. When larger vessels are treated, they may temporarily appear darker — this usually resolves within 4 weeks as the body reabsorbs the damaged vessels.
  5. Following pigmentation treatments, the treated lesion may appear darker immediately after treatment — this is normal. Over the following days it may form microcrusts which will flake away naturally. Do not exfoliate the area and allow the microcrusts to fall away on their own. Do not pick.
  6. Makeup can be applied after several hours if the skin is not sore or broken. We advise caution if the area feels sensitive. Remove makeup carefully — rubbing the skin can cause irritation or infection. If the skin is broken, avoid makeup until fully healed.
  7. Avoid trauma to the treated area for 4–5 days after treatment. This means avoiding strenuous exercise, saunas, steam rooms, excessively hot baths, massage, and tight clothing over the treated area.
  8. Wash and bathe as normal, but use cool showers rather than hot baths. Pat the skin dry — do not rub. Use Lynton Light Soothe serum to reduce irritation and keep the area moisturised. If there is any discomfort, treat the area as you would sunburn.
  9. Sun protection is essential. Use SPF50 (e.g. Lynton Light Protect Daily Defence) or SPF30+ to protect the area from sunlight between treatments and for at least one month after treatment ends. Failure to do this can lead to sunburn and hyperpigmentation of the treated area.
  10. If blistering occurs, contact the clinic immediately for further advice. Keep the area clean and dry. Do not use any lotions or creams on open skin as this may increase the risk of infection. Do not pick grazes or blisters — this may increase the chance of scarring. Once the skin has healed, use a high-protection sunscreen daily for 12 months to prevent pigmentation changes.

When to Contact Us

Please contact the clinic promptly if you experience any of the following after treatment:

  • Blistering or open wounds at the treatment site
  • Signs of infection — increasing redness, warmth, swelling, discharge, or fever
  • Severe or persistent pain that does not respond to cool compresses
  • Any symptoms that feel severe, unusual, or are worsening
