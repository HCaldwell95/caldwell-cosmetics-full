What Is illumiFacial®

Advanced skin rejuvenation with medical-grade IPL

illumiFacial® is an advanced, non-invasive skin rejuvenation treatment designed to restore brightness, clarity, and overall skin quality. Combining professional exfoliation with medical-grade IPL technology, the treatment targets multiple skin concerns simultaneously while leaving the skin visibly refreshed and radiant.

The treatment is ideal for improving pigmentation, redness, dullness, uneven skin tone, and early signs of ageing - all with minimal downtime and long-lasting skin benefits.

What to Expect

Your illumiFacial® experience

Before treatment, you'll have a detailed consultation with your practitioner to assess your skin condition, discuss your concerns, and determine the most suitable treatment approach.

This ensures the treatment is tailored specifically to your skin type, goals, and overall skin health.

  1. 1

    Deep Cleanse

    The skin is thoroughly cleansed to remove impurities, makeup, and oils before treatment begins.

  2. 2

    Tri-Fruit Acid Peel

    A gentle glycolic-based peel exfoliates the skin, refining texture and preparing the skin for IPL rejuvenation.

  3. 3

    Lynton IPL Application

    Medical-grade IPL targets pigmentation, redness, and signs of ageing while stimulating collagen production beneath the skin.

  4. 4

    Hydration & Protection

    Professional serums and SPF are applied to soothe, hydrate, and protect the skin after treatment.

Many clients notice brighter, smoother skin after just one session, with continued improvement developing over the following weeks as collagen production increases.

A course of treatments spaced 4–6 weeks apart is typically recommended for optimal long-term results.

{# ── 6. Aftercare ────────────────────────────────────────────────── #}

Aftercare

Protect and maintain your results

  • ● Some mild redness or sensitivity may occur and usually settles within a few hours.
  • ● Avoid direct sun exposure and wear SPF 30+ daily following treatment.
  • ● Use gentle skincare products for several days post-treatment.
  • ● Avoid harsh exfoliants, active acids, and retinol immediately after treatment.
  • ● Personalised aftercare guidance will be provided during your appointment.
