What Is Pigment & Vascular Removal

Advanced IPL treatment for clearer, more even-toned skin

Pigment & Vascular Removal uses advanced Intense Pulsed Light (IPL) technology to target unwanted pigmentation and visible vascular concerns within the skin.

By delivering carefully calibrated pulses of light, the treatment selectively targets melanin and haemoglobin beneath the skin's surface - helping to reduce pigmentation, redness, thread veins, and flushing while improving overall skin clarity and rejuvenation.

At Caldwell Cosmetics, we use the Lynton DYNAMIX® Medical-Grade IPL System, trusted by clinics across the UK for its safety, precision, and clinically effective results.

What to Expect

Your treatment journey

Before treatment begins, you'll have a detailed consultation to assess your skin concerns, medical history, and suitability for IPL therapy.

Your practitioner will identify the type of pigmentation or vascular concern being treated and create a personalised treatment plan tailored to your skin type and goals.

  1. 1

    Skin Preparation

    The treatment area is cleansed and prepared to ensure optimal IPL delivery and skin safety.

  2. 2

    IPL Application

    Pulses of light are delivered into the skin where the energy selectively targets unwanted pigment and visible blood vessels.

  3. 3

    Natural Breakdown Process

    The body gradually breaks down and reabsorbs the treated pigment or vascular lesion over the following days and weeks.

  4. 4

    Post-Treatment Care

    Cooling products and SPF are applied to soothe and protect the skin following treatment.

Pigmented areas may temporarily darken before naturally fading, while vascular concerns gradually reduce in visibility as the skin heals.

Most clients see progressive improvement over a course of treatments, with clearer, brighter, and more even-toned skin developing over time.

Aftercare

Protecting your skin post-treatment

  • ● Mild redness, warmth, or sensitivity may occur temporarily after treatment.
  • ● Avoid direct sun exposure before and after treatment wherever possible.
  • ● Wear SPF 30+ daily to protect the treated skin and maintain results.
  • ● Avoid harsh skincare products, active acids, and excessive heat immediately following treatment.
  • ● Personalised aftercare instructions will be provided during your appointment.
