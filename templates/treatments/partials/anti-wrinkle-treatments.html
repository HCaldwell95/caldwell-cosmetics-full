What Are Anti-Wrinkle Treatments

Subtle, natural facial rejuvenation

Anti-wrinkle treatments are designed to temporarily relax targeted facial muscles responsible for lines and wrinkles. The skin appears smoother, fresher, and more youthful - while still maintaining natural facial expression.

At Caldwell Cosmetics, every treatment is tailored specifically to your facial anatomy and aesthetic goals. Our approach focuses on refined, balanced enhancement - never a frozen or overdone appearance.

What to Expect

Your treatment journey

Before treatment, you'll have a full consultation with an experienced practitioner and prescriber. We assess your facial anatomy, muscle movement, medical history, and treatment goals to create a personalised plan.

This ensures safe treatment and natural-looking results tailored specifically to you.

  1. 1

    Assessment & Marking

    The treatment areas are assessed while you make natural facial expressions to identify precise injection points.

  2. 2

    Treatment

    Small, carefully placed injections are administered using advanced techniques designed for comfort and precision.

  3. 3

    Aftercare

    You'll receive detailed aftercare guidance before leaving the clinic.

{# ── 6. Aftercare ────────────────────────────────────────────────── #}

Aftercare

Important guidance following treatment

  • ● Do not rub, massage, or apply pressure to the treated area for at least 4 hours.
  • ● Avoid strenuous exercise, saunas, and excessive heat for 24 hours.
  • ● Remain upright for 4 hours following treatment.
  • ● Avoid alcohol immediately after treatment where possible.
  • ● Full aftercare advice will be provided during your appointment.

Duration & Maintenance

Maintaining your results

Anti-wrinkle treatment appointments usually take between 15-30 minutes depending on the areas treated.
