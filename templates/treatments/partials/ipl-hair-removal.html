What Is IPL Hair Removal

Advanced Intense Pulsed Light Technology

IPL (Intense Pulsed Light) hair removal uses precisely calibrated bursts of broad-spectrum light to target the melanin within each hair follicle. The light converts to heat, disabling the follicle's ability to produce new hair - progressively, over a course of sessions, delivering lasting smoothness.

At Caldwell Cosmetics we use the Lynton DYNAMIX® - a UK-exclusive, medical-grade IPL machine trusted by clinicians across the country. Unlike high-street devices, medical-grade IPL is calibrated to your exact skin and hair type, ensuring both safety and efficacy.

What to Expect

Your treatment journey

Before any treatment begins you'll have a thorough consultation with your practitioner. We assess your skin tone (Fitzpatrick scale), hair colour and thickness, and discuss your goals and medical history. A small patch test is performed to confirm your skin responds well.

This is your opportunity to ask questions and feel completely confident before committing to a course.

  1. 1

    Preparation

    The area is cleansed to remove any oils or residue. A cooling gel is applied to maximise your comfort throughout the procedure.

  2. 2

    IPL Application

    The Lynton DYNAMIX® handpiece glides across the skin, delivering calibrated pulses of light. Most clients describe the sensation as a gentle warmth or mild snap - nothing more.

  3. 3

    Post-Treatment

    A soothing moisturiser and SPF are applied. Any mild redness typically resolves within a couple of hours. You can return to normal activities immediately.

Hair growth reduces progressively after each session. Most clients notice a significant reduction after 3–4 sessions, with near-permanent results typically achieved after a full course of 6–8.

Hair colour and skin tone affect results - your practitioner will give you a realistic, honest expectation at consultation.

{# ── 6. Aftercare ─────────────────────────────────────────────────── #}

Aftercare

Protect your results

  • ● Avoid sun exposure and apply SPF 30+ to treated areas for at least two weeks post-treatment.
  • ● Do not wax, thread, or use depilatory creams between sessions - shaving is fine and will not affect results.
  • ● Avoid hot baths, saunas, and intense exercise for 24 hours following each session.
  • ● Mild redness or sensitivity is normal and typically resolves within a few hours.
  • ● You will receive a personalised aftercare sheet at your appointment to take home.
