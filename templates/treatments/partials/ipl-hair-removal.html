What Is IPL / Laser Hair Removal

Advanced Intense Pulsed Light Technology

Light-based technologies target and disable hair follicles, significantly reducing unwanted hair growth over time. As the treated follicles lose their ability to produce new hair, clients typically experience long-lasting smoothness and a dramatic reduction in the amount, thickness, and frequency of hair growth.

These treatments are suitable for a range of face and body areas and are performed over a series of sessions to effectively target hairs during their active growth cycle. While many treated follicles will no longer produce hair, hormonal changes, genetics, and natural biological processes can sometimes stimulate new hair growth in the future. For this reason, occasional maintenance treatments may be recommended to help maintain optimal long-term results.

Results vary between individuals, but most clients enjoy a substantial and lasting reduction in hair growth, making permanent hair reduction a convenient alternative to regular shaving, waxing, and plucking.

What to Expect

Your treatment journey

Before any treatment begins you'll have a thorough consultation with your practitioner. We assess your skin tone (Fitzpatrick scale), hair colour and thickness, and discuss your goals and medical history. A small patch test is performed to confirm your skin responds well.

This is your opportunity to ask questions and feel completely confident before committing to a course.

  1. 1

    Preparation

    Shave prior to appoint. The area is cleansed to remove any oils or residue. A cooling gel is applied to maximise your comfort throughout the procedure.

  2. 2

    IPL Application

    The Lynton DYNAMIX® handpiece glides across the skin, delivering calibrated pulses of light. Most clients describe the sensation as a gentle warmth or mild snap.

  3. 3

    Post-Treatment

    A soothing moisturiser and SPF are applied. Any mild redness typically resolves within a couple of hours. You will receive treatment advice and aftercare instructions.

Hair growth reduces progressively after each session. Most clients notice a significant reduction after 3-4 sessions.

Hair colour and skin tone affect results - your practitioner will give you a realistic, honest expectation at consultation.

{# ── 6. Aftercare ─────────────────────────────────────────────────── #}

Aftercare

Protect your results

  • ● Avoid sun exposure and apply SPF 30+ to treated areas for at least two weeks post-treatment.
  • ● Do not wax, thread, or use depilatory creams between sessions - shaving is fine and will not affect results.
  • ● Avoid hot baths, saunas, and intense exercise for 24 hours following each session.
  • ● Mild redness or sensitivity is normal and typically resolves within a few hours.
  • ● You will receive personalised aftercare at your appointment.
