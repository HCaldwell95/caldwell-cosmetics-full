Prescribed Homecare

Clinical skincare, tailored to you

What you do at home between clinic visits is just as important as what happens in the treatment room. At Caldwell Cosmetics, every client receives a personalised homecare programme — not a generic off-the-shelf routine, but a clinically prescribed selection of pHformula products chosen specifically for your skin type, concerns, and treatment plan.

pHformula's homecare range is formulated to paramedical standards, using the same evidence-based ingredient technology as their in-clinic treatments. This means your homecare actively supports and extends the results of your clinic sessions, rather than simply maintaining the status quo.

Whether you are managing acne, targeting pigmentation, slowing the signs of ageing, or rebuilding a compromised skin barrier, the right homecare prescription makes a measurable difference to your long-term results.

{# ── 3. Your Routine ───────────────────────────────────────────────── #}

Building Your Routine

The four pillars of prescribed skincare

Every effective skincare routine begins with thorough but gentle cleansing. pHformula cleansers are formulated to remove impurities, excess sebum, and environmental pollutants without stripping or disrupting the skin barrier.

Your prescribed cleanser will be matched to your skin type — whether that is a foaming formulation for oily and acne-prone skin, a cream cleanser to support sensitive or dry skin, or a purifying option for congestion-prone complexions.

The treatment step is where the most targeted work happens. Depending on your skin concerns, your homecare prescription will include one or more serums, boosters, or corrective formulas designed to address your specific goals — whether that is pigmentation, ageing, acne, or sensitivity.

pHformula's active treatment products use concentrated, clinically studied ingredients — including retinol, niacinamide, vitamin C complexes, AHAs, and barrier-repairing actives — selected and dosed precisely for your skin.

Hydration and barrier support are fundamental to any skin health goal. pHformula moisturisers are designed to deliver targeted hydration while reinforcing the skin barrier, supporting recovery after in-clinic treatments and maintaining skin comfort throughout your programme.

Your moisturiser will be chosen to complement your treatment step and suit your skin type — lightweight for oily or acne-prone skin, and richer formulations for dry or post-treatment skin.

Daily broad-spectrum SPF is non-negotiable — particularly when using active ingredients or undergoing resurfacing treatments. UV exposure is the single biggest driver of premature ageing and pigmentation, and protecting your skin daily ensures your results are preserved and built upon rather than undone.

pHformula's RESIST range provides high-factor, cosmetically elegant sun protection that can be worn comfortably every day. Your SPF will be prescribed as a core part of your homecare plan, regardless of skin type or concern.
