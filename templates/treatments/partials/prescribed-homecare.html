{# ── 2. Product Ranges ─────────────────────────────────────────────── #}

pHformula Homecare Ranges

Targeted solutions for every skin concern

A.C. Complex

Acne & Congestion

Formulated for acne-prone and congested skin, the A.C. homecare range helps regulate sebum production, reduce breakouts, and support healthy skin renewal. Products work to calm active acne while improving post-blemish marks and overall skin clarity.

Age Management Complex

Ageing & Firmness

Designed to target visible signs of ageing, including fine lines, loss of firmness, and uneven texture. This range supports collagen stimulation and skin renewal at home, helping to maintain and build on results achieved through in-clinic resurfacing treatments.

C.R. Complex

Sensitivity & Redness

A calming, barrier-supportive range developed for sensitive, reactive, and redness-prone skin. The C.R. homecare products help reduce visible redness, soothe inflammation, and strengthen the skin's natural defence barrier between clinic visits.

M.E.L.A Complex

Pigmentation & Uneven Tone

Targeted at hyperpigmentation, sun damage, melasma, and uneven skin tone. The M.E.L.A homecare range works to regulate melanin production and support a clearer, more even complexion - an essential companion to in-clinic pigmentation treatments.

Spot-On Treatment

Dark Spots & Uneven Tone

Spot-on treatments are targeted products designed to be applied directly to individual blemishes or areas of concern. They work by helping to reduce inflammation, calm redness, and support the skin's natural healing process. For best results, apply only to the affected area as directed and avoid overuse, as excessive application may cause dryness or irritation. Consistent use alongside your recommended skincare routine can help promote a clearer, healthier-looking complexion.

How Your Homecare is Prescribed

A clinical approach, not a retail one

  1. 1

    Skin Analysis

    Your skin type, concerns, and lifestyle are assessed at consultation using the pHformula SP Complex Skin Analysis framework - forming the foundation of your personalised prescription.

  2. 2

    Treatment Plan Alignment

    Your homecare products are selected to complement your in-clinic treatment plan - working synergistically to extend results, prepare the skin for upcoming sessions, and support recovery.

  3. 3

    Product Introduction

    Your prescribed routine is explained in detail at your appointment, including the correct application method, product layering order, and frequency. There are no guesses - you leave knowing exactly what to do and when.

  4. 4

    Ongoing Review

    As your skin responds and progresses, your homecare prescription is reviewed and adjusted at each visit. Your routine evolves with your skin, ensuring continued effectiveness throughout your treatment journey.
