PHFormula Skin Resurfacing

Targeted skin renewal with clinically proven results

PHFormula uses an innovative skin resurfacing delivery system to address a wide range of skin concerns — from signs of ageing and pigmentation to acne, congestion, and uneven texture. Every treatment begins with an advanced SP Complex Skin Analysis, allowing us to build a personalised treatment plan tailored precisely to your skin's needs.

Unlike traditional chemical peels, PHFormula works with the skin's biology rather than against it — supporting healthy cellular renewal while maintaining the skin barrier. The result is clearer, stronger, and more radiant skin with controlled, manageable recovery.

What PHFormula Can Improve

  • ✓Fine lines, wrinkles, and loss of skin firmness
  • ✓Pigmentation, sun damage, and uneven skin tone
  • ✓Active acne and congestion
  • ✓Uneven skin texture and dullness
  • ✓Sensitivity and compromised skin barrier

Before your first PHFormula treatment, an in-depth SP Complex Skin Analysis is carried out to assess your skin's condition, concerns, and goals. This forms the foundation of your personalised treatment plan, ensuring every session is precisely targeted to what your skin needs.

  1. 1

    Skin Preparation

    The skin is cleansed and prepped to optimise the penetration and effectiveness of the resurfacing treatment.

  2. 2

    Resurfacing Application

    The PHFormula resurfacing complex is precisely applied to the treatment areas. The formula and intensity are selected based on your personalised skin analysis.

  3. 3

    Neutralisation & Finishing

    The treatment is neutralised and supporting recovery products are applied to soothe, protect, and nourish the skin.

Your treatment course will be outlined at consultation based on your skin's needs. Results build progressively — most clients notice a meaningful improvement in skin clarity, texture, and radiance across the course.

A personalised homecare programme will be prescribed to support and extend your results between sessions. Avoid retinoids, exfoliating acids, and vitamin C serums for 1 week before treatment, and do not wax or have other facial treatments for 2 weeks prior.

Nanoneedling

Deeper serum infusion, zero downtime

Nanoneedling is a gentle, non-invasive treatment that uses a device tipped with hundreds of tiny pyramid-shaped silicone tips to create superficial micro-channels in the skin's surface. These channels allow active serums to penetrate far more deeply and effectively than topical application alone — dramatically improving ingredient absorption and treatment results.

It is an excellent standalone treatment for hydration, brightness, and skin tone, and can also be used to complement and enhance other treatments within a skin health programme. There is no downtime, and most clients find it very comfortable — often described as feeling like gentle sandpaper moving across the skin.

What Nanoneedling Can Improve

  • ✓Dehydration and dullness
  • ✓Uneven skin tone and early pigmentation
  • ✓Fine lines and loss of radiance
  • ✓Overall skin texture and smoothness
  • ✓Absorption of targeted active serums
  1. 1

    Cleanse & Prepare

    The skin is thoroughly cleansed and a targeted serum is selected and applied based on your skin's specific needs.

  2. 2

    Nanoneedling

    The nanoneedling device is gently moved across the face in a precise pattern, pushing the active serum deeper into the skin through thousands of micro-channels.

  3. 3

    Finishing Products

    A moisturiser and SPF are applied to seal in the treatment and protect the skin. You can return to your day immediately.

Some mild flushing may occur for a few hours following treatment — this is normal and settles quickly. Avoid makeup for 12–24 hours to allow the micro-channels to close fully.

Apply a gentle moisturiser and SPF 50+ in the days following treatment, and avoid exfoliating acids for 48 hours prior to your appointment. Inform your practitioner of any active skin conditions or sensitivities before the session.

Skin Plan Analysis

The most informed starting point for your skin

The Skin Plan Analysis is a comprehensive, standalone service for anyone who wants a clear, professional understanding of their skin before committing to a treatment pathway. Using professional clinical tools and in-depth assessment, we build a detailed picture of your skin's current condition — and create a fully personalised, evidence-based skincare and treatment plan around it.

This removes the guesswork entirely. Rather than trialling products and treatments that may not suit your skin, you'll leave with a written plan that tells you exactly where to invest your time and money for the results you want.

  1. 1

    In-Depth Skin Assessment

    Your skin is assessed using professional clinical tools, evaluating hydration, pigmentation, sensitivity, texture, pore health, and signs of ageing.

  2. 2

    Lifestyle & Routine Review

    We discuss your diet, sleep, stress levels, current skincare routine, and any medications or supplements that may be influencing your skin.

  3. 3

    Personalised Skin Plan

    A clear, written skin plan is created for you to take away — covering recommended treatments, product guidance, and realistic timelines.

You will leave with a written, personalised skin plan covering treatment recommendations, homecare product advice, and a realistic timeline for achieving your goals.

Optional follow-up reviews at 3–6 months are available to track your progress and refine the plan as your skin evolves. To get the most accurate assessment, please arrive with clean skin and no makeup — and bring a list of your current skincare products if possible.
