How to Book a Treatment

  1. Click the Book Now! link

    This will take you to our external booking platform, Aesthetic Nurse Software

  2. Book an Appointment

    Click on "Schedule a Treatment" button.

  3. Select Your Treatment

    Click on the treatment you wish to book, read the brief description to ensure you select the right service.

  4. Choose Your Preferred Date & Time

    The calendar will show available slots in real time. Select a date that suits you and a specific time from the options displayed.

  5. Provide Your Details

    Fill in your full name, email address, and contact number. If it's your first booking, you may need to create a quick account with a password. Some treatments may require additional information (e.g., medical history, skin concerns).

  6. Payment

    Some treatments may require payment upfront or a deposit. Follow the secure payment instructions. You will receive a confirmation receipt once your payment is successful.

  7. Confirmation & Reminders

    You will receive an email and/or SMS confirmation with your booking details when the appointment has been confirmed. The system may send reminders 24-48 hours before your appointment. If you need to reschedule or cancel, call text or email.

  8. Tips for a Smooth Booking Experience

    Ensure you have a stable internet connection when booking. Check your spam/junk folder if you don't see your confirmation email.

It's a quick and easy process!
