Anti-Wrinkle Treatments

Overview

At Caldwell Cosmetics, our Anti-Wrinkle Treatments are designed to smooth and rejuvenate your skin, reducing the appearance of fine lines and wrinkles for a more youthful and refreshed look. Using advanced techniques and high-quality products, our treatments effectively address common signs of ageing with precision and care.

What to Expect

Consultation:

Prior to your Anti-Wrinkle Treatment, you will have a thorough consultation with a prescriber. This will involve assessing your skin, discussing your aesthetic goals, and customising a treatment plan to achieve the best results for your individual needs.

Treatment Steps:

  1. Preparation: The treatment area will be cleansed.
  2. Injection: Advanced techniques are used to administer our anti-wrinkle solution, targeting specific areas where fine lines and wrinkles are most prominent.
  3. Post-Treatment Care: A soothing moisturiser and SPF may be applied to protect your skin and enhance recovery.

Benefits:

  • Reduction of Fine Lines and Wrinkles: Diminishes the appearance of fine lines and wrinkles, particularly around the forehead, eyes, and mouth.
  • Natural-Looking Results: Smooth, natural-looking results while preserving facial expressions.
  • Quick and Convenient: Minimally invasive with little to no downtime.
  • Preventative Care: Helps prevent new wrinkles and maintain a youthful appearance over time.

Ideal For:

Suitable for individuals seeking to address visible signs of ageing, including crow’s feet, frown lines, and forehead wrinkles.

Aftercare:

  • Do not rub or massage the treated area for 4 hours after treatment.
  • Avoid strenuous exercise for 24 hours.
  • Remain upright for 4 hours; avoid lying on your front.
  • Avoid tight-fitting headwear across the forehead.
  • Minimise excessive alcohol and consult about blood-thinning medications before treatment.

Duration and Frequency:

The session usually takes 15-30 minutes. Follow-up treatments every 3-6 months are recommended for best results.
