What Is PRP Treatment

Natural regeneration using your body’s own growth factors

Platelet Rich Plasma (PRP) therapy is an advanced regenerative treatment that uses your own blood to stimulate natural healing, collagen production, and tissue regeneration.

At Caldwell Cosmetics, we use a high-quality gel separator system to achieve a concentrated, pure PRP solution - maximising the effectiveness of each treatment for both skin rejuvenation and hair restoration.

This treatment is widely regarded as one of the most natural and biocompatible ways to improve skin quality, texture, and overall vitality.

{# ── 3. What to Expect ───────────────────────────────────────────── #}

What to Expect

Your PRP treatment journey

Before treatment, you will have a detailed consultation to assess your skin or hair concerns, medical history, and suitability for PRP therapy.

Your practitioner will design a fully personalised treatment plan based on your goals and the area being treated.

  1. 1

    Blood Collection

    A small sample of blood is taken from your arm in a safe and controlled clinical environment.

  2. 2

    Centrifugation Process

    The blood is spun in a centrifuge to separate platelet-rich plasma from other blood components.

  3. 3

    PRP Preparation

    The concentrated PRP is carefully extracted and prepared for precise application.

  4. 4

    Application

    PRP is injected into targeted areas using fine needles to stimulate natural regeneration and healing.

  5. 5

    Post-Treatment Care

    PPP is gently massaged into the treated area, and aftercare guidance is provided to optimise healing.

Results develop gradually as collagen production and tissue regeneration increase over time.

Most clients notice improvement after 4–6 weeks, with optimal results achieved after a course of treatments.

{# ── 6. Aftercare ────────────────────────────────────────────────── #}

Aftercare

Supporting optimal healing and results

  • ● Mild redness, swelling, or bruising may occur and usually resolves within a few days.
  • ● Avoid touching or applying pressure to the treated area for several hours post-treatment.
  • ● Avoid intense exercise, heat, and alcohol for 24 hours following treatment.
  • ● Follow a gentle skincare routine and avoid active ingredients immediately after treatment.
  • ● Full aftercare guidance will be provided during your appointment.
{# ── 7. Duration & Frequency ─────────────────────────────────────── #}

Duration & Frequency

Building results over time

A typical PRP session lasts between 60–90 minutes depending on the treatment area.

For optimal results, a course of treatments spaced 4–6 weeks apart is usually recommended. Your practitioner will tailor this plan based on your individual needs and goals.
