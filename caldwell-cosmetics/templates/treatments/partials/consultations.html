What to Expect

Your consultation experience

Your consultation begins with an open, one-to-one discussion about your goals, concerns, and expectations. We take the time to understand what you'd like to achieve and answer any initial questions you may have.

Our approach is always honest, professional, and focused on achieving results that enhance your natural features.

  1. 1

    Medical & Lifestyle Review

    We review your medical history, current skincare routine, and lifestyle factors to ensure treatment suitability and safety.

  2. 2

    Skin & Facial Assessment

    Your skin condition, facial anatomy, and areas of concern are carefully assessed to determine the most effective treatment approach.

  3. 3

    Treatment Recommendations

    Suitable treatments are explained in detail, including expected outcomes, timelines, maintenance, and aftercare considerations.

Following your assessment, we'll create a personalised treatment plan tailored specifically to your goals and priorities.

This may include recommended treatments, session frequency, skincare advice, and realistic timelines for achieving the best possible results.
